Biso nyonso toyebi ndenge ezalaka kosenga kolimbola eloko oyo tokoki kolimbola te. Mbote na bino nyonso bozali kotanga na Lingala — bisika nyonso bozali, nazali na esengo ete bozali awa.

Yesu alekaki mobali oyo akufaki miso kobanda kobotama na ye. Bayekoli na ye batunaki, "Moteyi, nani asalaki masumu, mobali oyo, to baboti na ye, ete abotamaki mokufi miso?" Yesu alobaki, "Mobali oyo asalaki masumu te, to baboti na ye te: kasi mpo na ete misala ya Nzambe emonana na ye."

Sima ataki mate na mabele, asalaki potopoto na mate, mpe apakolaki miso ya mokufi miso. Alobaki na ye, "Kende, sokola na etima ya Siloamu." Akendaki, asokolaki, mpe azongaki komona.

Bafalisi batunaki ye mpe, mpe balobaki ete Yesu akokaki kozala te kobanda na Nzambe, zambi abikisaki na Mokolo ya Pemi. Mobali alobaki, "Eloko moko nayebi, ete, ata nazalaki mokufi miso, sikawa namoni."

Babwakaki ye libanda. Yesu amonaki ye mpe alobaki, "Ondimi na Mwana ya Nzambe?" Alobaki, "Nkolo, nandimi." Mpe asambelaki ye.

Mpo na nini okanisi bayekoli batunaki nani azalaki na masumu mpo na kokufa miso? Nini okoloba soki moto atunaki yo nini Yesu asalaki mpo na yo? Mpe eyano ya mobali — "Nazalaki mokufi miso, mpe sikawa namoni" — elobi nini na yo?

Soki ozali kotanga oyo na Kongo — to bisika nyonso na mokili esika otangaka Lingala — okoki kopesa mwa tango mpo na kopesa mbote na Jonathan?

*Maloba ya Biblia awa ebongisami mpo na site oyo.*
